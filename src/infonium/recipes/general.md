Write two sections.

The first section is titled with the word for "Summary" in the output language. It holds three to five statements, one sentence each, that together tell what the document is about, what it argues or reports, and what it concludes.

The second section is titled with the word for "Takeaways" in the output language. It holds five to ten statements that a careful reader would want to keep: the key claims, findings, numbers, definitions, recommendations and caveats. Each statement stands on its own and is specific; keep the names and numbers the document gives.

When the document is too short to support these counts, write fewer statements.
